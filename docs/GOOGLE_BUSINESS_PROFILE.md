# Google Business Profile Setup

## Why It Matters
Google Business Profile (GBP) is the #1 factor for local SEO. It determines whether you appear in:
- Google Maps results
- Local "3-pack" (top 3 results for "real estate agent near me")
- Google AI Overviews for local queries

## Setup Steps

### 1. Create Your Profile
1. Go to https://business.google.com
2. Sign in with a Google account (create one for the business if needed)
3. Click "Add business"
4. Business name: `Harbison Standard`
5. Business category: `Real Estate Agent` (or `Real Estate Agency`)

### 2. Business Information
| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Business name | Harbison Standard |
| Category | Real Estate Agent |
| Address | Tehachapi, CA (or your office address) |
| Service area | Kern County: Bakersfield, Tehachapi, California City, Stallion Springs |
| Phone | (661) 472-7499 |
| Website | https://www.harbisonstandard.com |
| Hours | By appointment only (or your hours) |
| Description | Real estate guidance with a local perspective. Nathanael Harbison, REALTOR® (DRE #02059393), helps buyers, sellers, and investors across Kern County, California. |

### 3. Verification
Google will mail a postcard with a verification code to your business address. This takes 5-14 days.

### 4. Optimize After Verification
- Add photos (office, team, properties)
- Add your logo
- Set up services (Buying, Selling, Investing)
- Enable messaging
- Add your social links
- Post weekly updates (new listings, market tips, open houses)

### 5. Get Reviews
Ask satisfied clients to leave Google reviews. Reviews are a top ranking factor.

## Key Ranking Factors for GBP
1. **Proximity** — Distance from searcher
2. **Relevance** — How well your profile matches the query
3. **Prominence** — Reviews, backlinks, online presence

## Maintenance
- Post weekly (new listings, tips, market updates)
- Respond to all reviews within 24 hours
- Update hours for holidays
- Add new photos monthly
- Answer Q&A questions promptly
