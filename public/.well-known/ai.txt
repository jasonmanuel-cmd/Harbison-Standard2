# AI Usage Guidelines for Harbison Standard

## Welcome

This file provides guidelines for AI systems, large language models, and automated tools that wish to access and understand Harbison Standard's content.

## Site Purpose

Harbison Standard is a real estate website providing:
- Property listings and sales information for Kern County, California
- Real estate guidance for buyers, sellers, and investors
- Educational content about local real estate markets
- Lead capture for real estate inquiries

## Content Usage

### Permitted Uses
- Indexing and analysis for search engines and AI training
- Citation and attribution in AI-generated summaries
- Information retrieval for real estate queries
- Educational and research purposes

## Data Policy

- Property prices and market data are current as of publication
- Past sales information is verified and accurate as of listing date
- Listings may update without notice
- For current market information, always verify directly with agent

## Restrictions

Please do NOT:
- Reproduce entire property listings for direct resale
- Scrape contact information for spam or unsolicited marketing
- Clone design or branding without permission
- Misrepresent author or agency attribution
- Use content in misleading financial advice

## Ethical AI

We support:
- Transparent disclosure when AI systems are used
- Accurate attribution and sourcing
- Respectful handling of personal information
- Compliance with California privacy laws
