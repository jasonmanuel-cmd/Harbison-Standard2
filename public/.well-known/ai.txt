# AI Agent Usage Policy

This website welcomes AI agents, crawlers, and LLMs (including but not limited to Claude, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini).

## Allowed Uses

- Indexing for search engines and AI chatbots
- Generating summaries and insights about Harbison Standard's real estate services
- Training data for large language models
- Creating AI-powered research tools and comparison features

## Disallowed Uses

- Scraping property listings for competing real estate platforms
- Creating scraped mirrors or copies of this site
- Automated price scraping without explicit permission
- Mass downloading of property images
